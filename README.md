# Digital Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project evaluates digital marketing campaign performance using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focuses on advertising engagement, conversion performance, advertising costs, and return on advertising spend to understand campaign effectiveness and identify opportunities for budget optimization.

Key performance indicators, including Click-Through Rate (CTR), Cost per Click (CPC), Conversion Rate, and Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), are used to compare campaign performance and support data-driven marketing decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate campaign effectiveness using engagement, conversion, and revenue-related metrics.
- Compare campaign performance using CTR, CPC, Conversion Rate, and ROAS.
- Identify campaigns that generate stronger returns relative to advertising expenditure.
- Identify opportunities to improve campaign efficiency and budget allocation.

## Tools & Methods

**Primary Tool:** Microsoft Excel

- Data exploration and preparation
- Marketing KPI calculation
- Click-Through Rate (CTR) analysis
- Cost per Click (CPC) analysis
- Conversion Rate analysis
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) analysis
- Campaign performance comparison
- Data summarization and visualization

## Key Insights

- Retargeting Web recorded a CTR of 5.50%, a Conversion Rate of approximately 4.01%, and a ROAS of approximately 6.86x.
- Lookalike Audience recorded the lowest ROAS among the compared campaigns, at approximately 3.70x.
- Comparing engagement, conversion, and advertising returns provides a more comprehensive view of campaign effectiveness than evaluating clicks or impressions alone.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the strategies and audience characteristics associated with Retargeting Web's stronger performance.
- Review the targeting, creative materials, and conversion journey of campaigns with lower ROAS.
- Consider reallocating budget toward better-performing campaigns after evaluating spending levels, campaign objectives, and available performance data.
- Monitor CTR, CPC, Conversion Rate, and ROAS together to balance audience engagement, acquisition costs, and advertising returns.
